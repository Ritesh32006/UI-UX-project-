# 🚗 DriveEase – Car Rental Website UI/UX Design

## 📌 Project Overview

DriveEase is a modern, responsive car rental website UI/UX design created to provide users with a simple, convenient, and user-friendly car booking experience. The design helps users explore available cars, compare prices, and find suitable vehicles for their journeys.

This project is developed as part of a college UI/UX design assignment using Figma.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Design an attractive and professional car rental landing page.
* Provide a simple and intuitive user experience.
* Make the design suitable for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
* Use Figma Auto Layout and reusable components.
* Maintain consistent colors, typography, and spacing.

## 👥 Target Audience

* Travelers and tourists.
* Families planning trips.
* Students and working professionals.
* People looking for short-term car rentals.

## 🎨 Design System

**Color Palette**

* Navy Blue: `#0B1F3A`
* White: `#FFFFFF`
* Orange: `#FF8C42`

**Typography:** Inter or a similar modern sans-serif font.

**Design Style:** Minimal, modern, clean, and professional.

## ✨ Website Sections

1. **Navigation Bar** – Logo, navigation links, login, and booking button.
2. **Hero Section** – Main headline, description, car image, and call-to-action button.
3. **Car Search** – Pickup location, dates, times, and car type.
4. **Popular Cars** – Vehicle images, names, ratings, features, and rental prices.
5. **Why Choose Us** – Key benefits and services.
6. **How It Works** – Simple car booking steps.
7. **Promotional Banner** – Special offers and booking call to action.
8. **Customer Testimonials** – Reviews and ratings.
9. **Footer** – Quick links, contact information, and social media icons.
